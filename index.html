01 — ICI bargaining in Ontario

A province-wide framework. Local conditions negotiated here.

In 1978 the Ontario Labour Relations Act set out a legislated, province-wide structure for collective bargaining between employers and 28 unions in Ontario's ICI construction industry.

While the framework is provincial, many of those collective agreements contain local conditions specific to the region where the work is performed. Negotiating and administering those conditions is the responsibility of experienced construction association staff.

In Northwestern Ontario, that responsibility sits with NOLRO. Our staff know the local trade conditions in detail and work on behalf of contractors to apply them fairly and consistently, project after project.

The ICI sector we work in

Industrial, commercial and institutional construction across the North — each with its own trades, schedules and local conditions.

01 Industrial Mills, mines, generating stations and processing plants across the northwestern districts.
02 Commercial Offices, retail and mixed-use buildings, from downtown cores to regional centres.
03 Institutional Hospitals, schools, colleges and public facilities serving northwestern communities.
